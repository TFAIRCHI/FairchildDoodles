About Us

The story behind Fairchild Golden Doodles

Our Story

Fairchild Golden Doodles started as a dream born from a deep love of dogs. What began as a family passion quickly grew into a mission — to bring thoughtfully bred, healthy, and well-socialized Golden Doodles into the lives of loving families.

We are not a large-scale operation. We are a small, family-run breeder based right here in our community, where every puppy is whelped, raised, and cared for under our direct supervision. That means each litter gets the individual attention it deserves.

Over the years, we have refined our breeding program with one goal in mind: producing puppies that are not only beautiful, but healthy, confident, and eager to bond with their new families.

Our Breeding Philosophy

Every decision we make is guided by the health and wellbeing of our dogs

🧬

Genetic Testing

All breeding dogs undergo comprehensive genetic testing to screen for hereditary conditions common in Golden Retrievers and Poodles. We only breed dogs that come back with clear or manageable results.

🩺

Health Clearances

Our breeding dogs receive full OFA and CERF health clearances. We believe in transparency — full health records for every parent dog are available upon request.

🧠

Early Neurological Stimulation

From day 3 through day 16, each puppy undergoes Early Neurological Stimulation (ENS). Research shows this builds stronger immune systems, better temperament, and greater confidence.

🐕

Thoughtful Pairing

We carefully select breeding pairs based on health, temperament, coat type, and size — not just appearance. Each pairing is planned months in advance to produce the healthiest possible litter.
