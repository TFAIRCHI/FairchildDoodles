Allergy-Friendly Doodles

Understanding coat types, shedding, and what “hypoallergenic” really means

F1B — Very Good for Allergies

Why F1B Golden Doodles are a common recommendation for allergy‑sensitive families

  • 75% Poodle / 25% Golden Retriever
  • Most puppies are curly or wavy with minimal shedding
  • Common recommendation for allergy families
  • Excellent balance of temperament + allergy‑friendly coat

Important Note

No dog is 100% hypoallergenic, but F1B Golden Doodles are among the best options for families with allergies.

We encourage allergy‑sensitive families to schedule a visit and spend time with our dogs before reserving a puppy.
