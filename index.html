Welcome to the Family

At Fairchild Doodles, every puppy is raised as part of our family. We specialize in premium Doodles — combining the intelligence of Poodles with the loving nature of Golden Retrievers.

Our puppies are health-tested, socialized from birth, and raised in a nurturing home environment. Each one comes with complete health records, genetic testing, and a lifetime of breeder support.
